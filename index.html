How to use this calculator

A prescriber-facing tool for converting cannabis-experienced patients from inhaled flower onto a Can Labs extract, and for anchoring Naïve and Breakthrough titration plans. Walk through the steps in order — the page updates as you type.

  1. Pick the patient category on the left: Experienced (converting from inhaled flower), Naïve (no recent cannabis), or Breakthrough / PRN (rescue dosing on top of a stable baseline).
  2. Pick the Can Labs product you're prescribing. The Total THC field fills in with the spec-nominal value.
  3. Overwrite the Total THC (mg/g) with the measured value from the Certificate of Analysis for the specific batch the patient will be dispensed. Type the batch reference into the box below it.
  4. Only if "Experienced": enter the patient's current flower %THC, mass per session, and sessions per day.
  5. Read the Week-1 plan with the patient (highlighted in pink): starting dose per session, sessions per day, and what that represents as a percentage of their prior daily THC. Discuss tolerability expectations.
  6. Print the page for the patient and for the medical record. The printout pins the product, batch reference, and date.

This is a decision-support tool — it does not set a maximum daily dose. Clinical judgement, red-flag screening, and review triggers (see panels below) govern every step.

Calculated outputs (per session)

Clinical note: Week 1 targets ≈50% of the patient's prior daily THC exposure as a conversion safety factor. Because the practical minimum per-session extract dose (0.05 g) is denser than typical flower sessions, the calculator reduces sessions/day rather than the per-dose mass to honour the safety factor. Single-variable escalation only — never change two variables at once.
